ABOUT THIS VIEW

Election information with source transparency.

The dashboard combines supplied constituency, Senate, state and LGA boundary files with a polling-unit listing and supplemental geocodes. 2019/2023 fields are historical summaries. No 2027 election result or voter register is represented.

Map points have different confidence levels. Inspect each polling unit’s coordinate_source and location_status attributes. Estimated points are not precise polling locations.
